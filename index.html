1. Apparent Heat Index (HI) — Rothfusz Polynomial
HI = -8.7847 + 1.6114·T + 2.3385·(0.01H)·T - 0.1461·(0.01H)²·T - 0.0123·T² + ...

Computes human evaporative heat balance based on Steadman's biometeorological model.

2. Wet-Bulb Temperature (Tw) — Stull Empirical Equation
Tw = T·atan(0.151977√(H+8.31)) + atan(T+H) - atan(H-1.67) + 0.003918·H^1.5·atan(0.0231·H) - 4.686

Accurately computes adiabatic saturation temperature across ambient surface conditions.

Heatstroke Diagnostic & Field Protocol

Medical Emergency: Act within 15 minutes
Heat Exhaustion
  • Heavy sweating, cold clammy skin
  • Dizziness, nausea, faint pulse
  • Normal core body temperature
  • Action: Rest in shade, drink ORS water
Heatstroke (CRITICAL)
  • Hot, red, completely dry skin (sweating stopped)
  • Confusion, slurred speech, unconsciousness
  • Core body temp > 40°C (104°F)
  • Action: Call 112/108; pack ice in armpits & neck
🌊

Urban Flooding & Submergence Protocol

Key survival steps during sudden inundation
  • Turn Around, Don't Drown: Just 15cm (6 inches) of moving water can sweep a pedestrian off their feet; 30cm can float a compact vehicle.
  • Submerged Manhole Risk: Flood waters dislodge sewer manhole covers. Never wade through brown water without a sounding stick.
  • Electrical Disconnection: If ground floor water enters within 15cm of sockets, turn off the main double-pole isolator on upper floor.
  • Stagnant Water Vector Warning: Disinfect standing water within 48 hours to halt Dengue and Leptospirosis outbreaks.
